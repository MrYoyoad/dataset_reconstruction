## Setting

A frozen layer `h ↦ W₀h` (`W₀ ∈ R^{m×n}`) gets LoRA `W₀ + sBA`, `A ∈ R^{r×n}`, `B ∈ R^{m×r}`, with
`A₀` Gaussian iid and `B₀ = 0`. Private data as the layer sees it: `H = [h₁ … h_N]`, `hᵢ = φ(xᵢ)` for a
frozen public encoder φ. The attacker receives `(A_T, B_T)`, `W₀`, φ, the recipe (optimizer, η, T, σ₀) and the
conditioning (labels/captions); never `A₀`, `H`, or the trajectory. Standing assumptions: same `H` throughout
training (no LoRA dropout / augmentation / trainable upstream), `B₀=0`, an SGD-class update rule, `rank P_T = N`,
`r ≥ N+1`.

## The organizing statement (Rev 10 Theorem 1, span-adapted normal form)

With `U, V` orthonormal bases of `H = col H` and its complement and `X := A₀U`, `Y := A₀V`:
the whole trajectory is a measurable function of `(X, H, W₀, recipe)` — `Y` is never touched;
`A_T = XΩUᵀ + c_T Y Vᵀ`, `B_T = P_T Xᵀ`. In span-adapted coordinates the release is
**(deformed X) ⊕ (untouched Gaussian noise Y)**. Consequences: which coordinates carry private
information (`XΩ` and `C`), which are pure noise (`Y`), and what is unavoidably hidden (the training
span and `ker A₀`).

## Three primitives

1. **Quotient certificate.** `C := P_{row(B_T)^⊥} A_T` satisfies `CH = 0` exactly, `rank C = r − N`,
   `ker C = H ⊕ ker A₀`. Off the span it is exact Gaussian sensing. Survives momentum, decay, minibatching,
   and Muon (matrix maps `M ↦ p(MMᵀ)M`); dies under coordinatewise-nonlinear optimizers (Adam, RMSProp,
   Lion, signSGD) and LoRA dropout; deleted by a balanced/merged release. Also gives a membership score
   with a closed-form `χ²_{r−N}` null, a threshold-free span estimator `Ĥ = row(P_{row(B_T)} A_T)`, and a
   conservation-law inequality (`A_TA_Tᵀ − B_TᵀB_T = c_T²A₀A₀ᵀ`) that is the only released-only test
   sensitive to `ker A₀` contamination.
2. **Manifold pull-back.** `L_x := C J_φ(x) U_x`; `rank L_x = min(r − N, k − d_∥)` a.s. for each fixed x.
   Recovery from `C` alone works iff the manifold dimension `k < r − N` strictly — a sharp phase boundary
   (verified: ≈100% below the line, exactly 0% above it with residual 1e-14 true aliases, 0.67–0.9 on it).
3. **Exact inversion (the interesting one).** Do not assume representer coefficients — simulate the recipe.
   Solve `Recipe_T(φ(ψ(wᵢ)), X) = (B_T, A_T U)` for the latents and `X`: `mr + rN` equations for `Nk + rN`
   unknowns, cross-example mixing reproduced because the simulator *is* the training. The approximate
   (assumed-coefficient) representer is dead beyond a few percent deformation; the exact form reached
   1e-13…1e-16 at deformation up to ≈1.0 with finite-difference Jacobians, **but only from starts within
   ~10–15% of the truth** (30% off → local minimum).

## The crux this repo is now attacking

The **basin problem**, not identifiability: the exact inversion converges to machine precision where the
certificate is completely blind (`k > r − N`), provided it starts inside the basin. What the theory says a
learned decoder / population prior has to supply is therefore *an initializer*, not a metric and not a
gradient bridge. Open items: (a) how large the basin actually is with true backprop instead of finite
differences, (b) whether attacker-available initializers (span estimator, certificate anchor) enlarge it,
(c) whether an **Adam** release — where no certificate exists and all of `A₀` is unknown, count still
overdetermined (`mr + rn` vs `Nk + rn`) — is invertible at all, and (d) the (N,k) phase diagram of exact
inversion vs the C-only one (prediction: recovery on *both* sides of `k = r − N`).

## How this connects to the thesis

The framework is the theory-side counterpart of the repo's Direct Weight Inversion axis (CLAUDE.md primary
axis): DWI in the toy = exact inversion with the recipe as the forward model, here with an added exact
algebraic channel (`C`) that DWI does not use. It reframes the Gradient Bridge: a decoder trained on proxy
data is not needed to supply gradients, it is needed to supply a *starting point*. It also gives the
release-only diagnostics (span angles, `χ²_{r−N}` membership null, conservation margin) that the
`q_eff`/identifiability program has been looking for on the theory side, and it names the exact defenses
(canonical/balanced merge, Adam, LoRA dropout) that close the algebraic channel.
