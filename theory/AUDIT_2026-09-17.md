## F1 — T2 Proposition A: the mathematics is CONFIRMED (register: derived)

I re-derived the closure independently before reading the proof, and reached the same three invariants by the same
induction. Sketch of my derivation, for comparison:

- The `A`-update is `-eta s B_t^T D_t H_t^T`, whose **row space** lies in `col(H_t) subset Hcal`. Hence `A_t V' =
  A_0 V'` for any basis `V'` of `Hcal^perp`: the off-span block is never touched.
- `B_0 = 0`, so the `A`-update at `t=0` vanishes and `A_1 = A_0`. Inductively the `B`-update's **rows** lie in
  `col(A_t H_t) subset col(A_0 Hcal)`, and the `A`-update's **columns** lie in `sum_t row(B_t) subset
  col(A_0 Hcal)`. So `row(B_T) subset col(X')` and `col(A_T U') subset col(X')`, with `X' = A_0 U'`.
- Given `rank B_T = N'`, containment plus equal dimension forces `row(B_T) = col(X')`, so `P_{row(B_T)^perp}`
  annihilates `col(A_T U')`, and since every `H_{l,t}` has columns in `Hcal`, `C_full H_{l,t} = 0` for all `t`.
  `H_{l,0} = H_l^0` because `B_{l',0} = 0` at every upstream layer, so the base representation is the `t=0` member.

**This is the same theorem as W1's certificate theorem with `Hcal` replacing `col(H)` and `N'` replacing `q`.** The
substitution is exact, which is a strong consistency signal: two files derived independently reach the same closure.

## F2 — T2 Prop. A's stated RANK FORMULA IS WRONG, and contradicts Prop. A's own kernel formula

Prop. A states `rank C_full = r - N'`. That is false whenever `n_l < r`, and it disagrees with the *other*
conclusion of the same proposition. From `ker C_full = Hcal (+) ker A_0` and `rank A_0 = min(r, n_l)`:

    rank C_full = n_l - N' - max(0, n_l - min(r,n_l)) = max(0, min(r - N', n_l - N')).

| r | n_l | N' | stated `r-N'` | from Prop. A's own kernel | measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | 16 | 3 | **21** | 13 | **13** |
| 24 | 20 | 15 | **9** | 5 | **5** |
| 6 | 20 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 6 | 20 | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The stated form is wrong in **both exercised rows of the load-bearing check**. The proof's justification —
"because `A_0` is a.s. of full rank" — is the slip: `A_0` is `r x n_l`, so full rank means `min(r, n_l)`, not `r`.

This is not cosmetic. **The track's headline is that what depth costs is rank**, so the rank law is the headline
quantity, and the stated form overstates surviving rank by `r - n_l` on any layer narrower than the adapter rank.
The harness already uses `max(0, min(r-N', dims[l]-N'))` and the "certificate lifetime `T >= min(r,n_l)/N`" result
also uses `min(r,n_l)` — so **the code and one of the file's own results are right while the theorem statement is
wrong.** Fix the statement; nothing downstream needs to move.

**Status consequence:** T2 is marked PROVED. Prop. A's structural content is proved; its rank clause as written is
not, and should not travel in a Gal-facing document in its current form.

## F3 — the hypothesis list is TIGHTER than it looks (register: derived)

`row(B_T) subset col(X')` holds unconditionally and `dim col(X') <= N'`. So **B3 (`rank B_T = N'`) forces
`dim col(X') = N'`, i.e. `A_0` injective on `Hcal`.** The genericity condition one would expect to need separately
— and which W1 needed as an explicit hypothesis — is *implied* here. Worth stating: it makes B3 carry more than it
appears to, and it means no extra generic-position assumption is missing.

Correspondingly, B6's stated role ("`ker C_full = Hcal (+) ker A_0`") is only half right: the **directness** of the
sum comes from B3, and B6 is needed for the *dimension* of `ker A_0`, hence for the rank count in F2.

## F5 — T5.1 is CONFIRMED and it answers a question W1 records as OPEN (register: derived)

`Phi_l^0 = psi_l . Phi_{l-1}^0` gives `M_l = J_{psi_l} M_{l-1}`, so every block of `J_F` factors through `M_1`;
hence `ker M_1 subset ker J_F` and `rank J_F <= rank M_1 <= min(k, n_1)`. I derived this independently and it is
correct.

**Why it matters beyond this track:** W1 §6 lists the stacked-Jacobian rank across layers as *unmeasured*, and that
is precisely the third of the three counts — the only one that bears on identifiability. T5.1 supplies a proved
**upper bound** for it: no amount of depth exceeds the first adapted layer's chart sensitivity. That is a direct,
proved answer to the shape of Gal's "~200 equations" objection — the ceiling is `k_1`, not `sum_l (r_l - N)`.
Subject to this audit being accepted, W1's open clause can be upgraded from "unmeasured" to "bounded above by
`rank M_1` (T5.1); the value within that bound is unmeasured".

**How this must travel, because the obvious phrasing does damage.** "No amount of depth exceeds the first adapted
layer's chart sensitivity" reads as a blocker on the depth route. **It is not.** `k_1 = rank M_1 <= k`, so the
ceiling sits *at* the attacker's own chart dimension, not below it: T5.1 says depth cannot buy you more than the
chart you chose to search, which is a statement about the chart, not a limit on depth. Depth remains the mechanism
that *reaches* that ceiling when a single layer's budget `r - N` falls short of it — which is exactly T5.3's second
regime. Relay it as **"the ceiling on stacked information is the first adapted layer's chart sensitivity `k_1`, and
`k_1` is at most the chart dimension `k`"**, never as "depth adds nothing".

T5.4 is correctly marked CONJECTURE, and its own caveat — that the honest object under drift is *effective* rank at
a stated tolerance, not rank — is the right one and is the kind of distinction that usually has to be forced.

## F6 — the trajectory/endpoint scope line, and what the attacker can actually check

Gal's 15 Sept note observes that the base model and the released adapter give the features at *start and endpoint*
but not the intermediate trajectory. `Hcal = sum_{t<T} col(H_{l,t})` is exactly an object of the trajectory, so
`N'` is not attacker-computable. T2's assumption table says this ("`rank B_T` is observable; `N'` is not"), which
is honest and should stay.

The sharper form, and the one to state: the attacker **can** see when the channel is *empty* — `rank C_full` is
computed from the release alone, and `rank C_full = 0` is visible. What they **cannot** see is whether a *nonempty*
`C_full` is exact or contaminated, because that is `rank B_T = N'` and they do not have `N'`. So the failure modes
split: rank death is attacker-visible, contamination is not. This is the same shape as W1's corrected excitation
clause — **computable-from-the-release and verifiable-from-the-release are different properties** — and the
certificate-lifetime result (`T >= min(r,n_l)/N`) is consequently a **defender-facing** statement.

# F11 — **T5.2 IS FALSE AS STATED**, and T5.3's planning number depends on it (register: derived + read-rows)

Status in the file: **PROVED**. It is not. Counterexample constructed by derivation, then confirmed numerically
(job **350996**, `experiments/multilayer_cert/t52_counterexample.py`): **0 of 12 seeds** match T5.2; the corrected
law matches **12 of 12**.

## Where the proof leaks

T5.2 claims `rank J_F = min(k_1, sum_l q_l)` a.s. The upper bound is right. The **equality** assumes the per-layer
row spaces `row(Ctil_l M_l)` spread independently inside `row(M_1)`. They cannot, and **T5.1 is the reason**:

    M_l = J_{psi_l} M_{l-1}  =>  row(M_1) ⊇ row(M_2) ⊇ ... ⊇ row(M_L).

`row(Ctil_l M_l) ⊆ row(M_l)`, and when `q_l = rank M_l` the containment is **equality** — the layer contributes
`row(M_l)` exactly. So two deep layers whose row spaces have collapsed to the same small subspace contribute *the
same subspace twice*. T5.2 counts them twice. Independence of `A_{l,0}` across layers does not help: the summands
are independently *oriented* only within a space they are already confined to.

## The corrected law

    rank J_F = min_j ( d_j + sum_{l<j} q_l ),    d_j := rank M_j,  j = 1..L+1,  d_{L+1} := 0.

Each term says: whatever the first `j-1` layers contributed, everything from layer `j` on is trapped inside
`row(M_j)`, of dimension `d_j`. T5.2 is the `j = 1` and `j = L+1` terms only — it omits every intermediate
constraint.

## The measurement

`d = [10, 3, 3]`, `q = [5, 3, 3]`, `k_1 = 10`, `r = 13`, `N = 8`, three adapted layers, twelve seeds:

| | T5.2 predicts | corrected predicts | measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| all 12 seeds | **10** | **8** | **8** |

The gap is not a tolerance artefact: it is an integer rank, identical across every seed.

## What this does to T5.3, which is a live planning number

T5.3's second regime — `r - N < k_1` implies `L >= k_1/(r-N)` adapted layers suffice generically — is derived from
T5.2 and **inherits the error**. It is true only when `rank M_l = k_1` for **every** adapted layer, i.e. when the
frozen path between adapted layers preserves the chart's rank. Where the network contracts rank between adapted
layers, the ceiling is `min_j(d_j + sum_{l<j} q_l)` and **adding layers past the point where `d_j` has collapsed
buys nothing at all**.

So the planning figure in circulation — *8 adapted layers for `k_1 = 128` at `r = 24, N = 8`* — is an **upper bound
on what depth can deliver, not an estimate**, and it is only attained if the frozen path is rank-preserving between
the adapted layers. That is a concrete, checkable precondition on the architecture: measure `rank M_l` at each
adapted layer before committing to a depth budget.

    huge = any(float(h.abs().max()) > 1e100 for rt in reps for h in rt)
